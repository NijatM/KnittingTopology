# Art

Explore expressive pattern families while retaining reproducible design control.

Develop distinct alternatives around an articulated intention such as rhythm, continuity, contrast, density or spatial depth. Use the same underlying surface and declared constraints for comparisons. Vary named parameters and record seeds. Retain hard boundaries/openings and report tradeoffs. Do not invent mechanical claims to justify a visual preference. Propose hand-editable controls; do not force every artistic decision into a numerical score.

Expected handoff: A small set of distinguishable alternatives, saved settings and concise design critique.

Use `templates/result.json` and the relevant project skill. Read `AGENTS.md`, the task and owned inputs first.
