# Mechanics

Frame load-related questions and prevent unsupported structural interpretations.

Require an explicit object/model, units, loads, supports and material assumptions before interpreting force or stress. Distinguish truss/network, membrane and shell models; distinguish N, N/m and Pa. Curvature and drawn flow are not stress. Equilibrium is not stability or capacity. For compression identify the mode and stabilizing mechanism. Use analytic checks and calibration/held-out validation where applicable. Keep v1 load intent separate from verified performance. Explain how changing topology/material orientation may invalidate an imported field.

Expected handoff: A model specification, verified calculation when in scope, or a precise statement of missing evidence.
