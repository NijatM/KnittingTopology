---
name: surface-field-design
description: Design and combine surface-attached spacing, direction, curvature and diffusion fields for geometric pattern generation.
---

Establish surface units, revision, tangent frames, boundaries and algorithm preconditions. Distinguish scalar spacing/density, tangent vectors, undirected line fields, cross fields, geometric metrics and mechanical tensors. Normalize only compatible quantities. Preserve course/wale labels where the textile interpretation requires them.

Compare or interpolate directions in compatible surface frames; handle sign equivalence and record singular or low-confidence regions. State how drawings become hard/soft constraints and how conflicts are resolved. For diffusion, record surface discretization, boundary conditions, time integration, parameters and seed; verify the implemented scheme rather than relying on visual plausibility.

Return field data, assumptions, algorithm/version and relevant diagnostics. Treat a mechanical field as mechanically derived only when its model and load case are supplied. Curvature and reaction-diffusion alone provide geometric or expressive signals.
