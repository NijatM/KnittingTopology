---
name: expressive-pattern-exploration
description: Explore artistic variants of surface patterns with reproducible controls and explicit visual intentions.
---

Name the expressive goal and hard geometric constraints. Generate a small number of meaningfully distinct alternatives; vary controlled parameters or construction rules and record seeds/settings. Keep the comparison surface and essential constraints consistent.

Explain differences using concrete visual properties such as spacing rhythm, directional continuity, hierarchy, contrast, layering and openings. Offer direct editing controls where a numerical objective would erase the designer's judgment. Preserve an intentionally irregular option when relevant rather than optimizing everything toward uniformity.

Report geometric failures or constraint tradeoffs without inventing mechanical advantages. A compelling pattern may remain exploratory; artistic merit does not require an unsupported scientific explanation.
